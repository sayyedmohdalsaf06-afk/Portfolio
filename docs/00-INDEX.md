2. **[`02 — Identity First`](./02-identity-first.md)** *(Governing)*

3. **[`03 — Interaction Philosophy`](./03-interaction-philosophy.md)** *(Governing)*
   - The behavioral DNA of the interface.
   - 8 Laws of Behavior: Still by default, Agency over spectacle, Everything resolves to clarity, Continuous spatial memory, Motion earns its place, Contextual honest cursor, No dead ends, Dual speed.
   - The Signature Mechanic: *Resolving into Focus*.

4. **[`04 — Visual Identity`](./04-visual-identity.md)** *(Governing)*
   - Typography: General Sans (Voice) + JetBrains Mono (Thinking/Annotation register).
   - Color: Paper & Ink (Light default `#F4F1EA` / `#16161A`) + Graphite (Dark `#17181C` / `#EDE9E0`) with Focus-Blue signal (`#2A46E0`).
   - Material: Matte paper detailed like a precision instrument (zero glassmorphism).
   - Signature visual device: **The Focus Mark** (viewfinder / registration brackets).

5. **[`05 — Design Bible`](./05-design-bible.md)**
   - Blueprint → Built system architecture and comprehensive design guide.
