# CAMPLX — Campus + Exchange + Community

> "Trade smarter on campus."

A verified campus-first marketplace and community platform for students to buy, sell, exchange, donate, and request items within their college community.

## Product Capabilities
- **Campus Marketplace:** Buy, sell, exchange, and donate items with campus-specific discovery.
- **Need-It Board:** Post requests for items needed across campus dorms and departments.
- **Campus Chat UI:** Realtime peer-to-peer negotiation interface (UI implemented; Supabase Realtime planned).
- **Notifications UI:** Activity feeds and trade updates (UI implemented; backend triggers planned).
- **Wishlist & Reservations:** Save and reserve items directly from verified student peers.
- **Sustainability:** Track reuse metrics to encourage circular economy on campus.
- **Verified Identity:** Geo-fenced to authenticated campus credentials.

## Engineering Architecture
- **Client:** React Native, Expo SDK 54, Expo Router, NativeWind, Reanimated, Moti, Zustand, TanStack Query.
- **Backend & Database:** Supabase (Auth, PostgreSQL, Storage, Edge Functions, Realtime).
- **Validation:** React Hook Form + Zod.

## Planned Intelligence Layer (Phase 4)
- Smart Listing Categorization & AI Listing Assistant
- Intelligent Need-It Matching
- Personalized Discovery & Smart Search
- AI-Assisted Sustainability Insights
